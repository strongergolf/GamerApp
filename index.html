Impact Geometry & Ball Flight — Reference

The StrongerGolf vocabulary, worded to keep front-of-mind that impact is a 3D event: every variable has a Vertical and a Horizontal component of one 3D vector. This grounds every model and tool in the app.

Definitions
Impact Plane VPlane · HPlane
The shape of the clubhead's motion through the bottom of the swing. VPlane = the "lie angle" of that motion (upright/flat, DTL). HPlane = its horizontal aim (overhead). Not to be confused with Path.
3D Path VPath · HPath
Direction the clubhead is moving at impact. VPath = Angle of Attack (up+/down−). HPath = left/right of target line.
3D Face VFace · HFace
Direction the face points at impact. VFace = Dynamic Loft (incl. shaft lean). HFace = open/closed vs target.
Spin Loft VDiff · HDiff
3D angle between Face and Path, along the D-plane. VDiff = VFace − VPath (drives launch & spin). HDiff = HFace − HPath (tilts the spin axis = curve).
Spin Axis
Tilt of the ball's spin axis vs the horizon — the curve. Left(−) / Right(+).
3D Launch VLaunch · HLaunch
Initial ball direction. Ball starts on the D-plane, 75–85% toward the Face (≈75% wedges, 85% driver), then curves off the path.
Gear Effect — off-centre strike (RH; mirror for LH)

Why predicted ("estimated") spin axis diverges from actual: contact location bends the result.

Toe contact
Spin axis tilts left → more hook/draw spin.
Heel contact
Spin axis tilts right → more fade/slice spin.
High on face
Decreases spin rate.
Low on face
Increases spin rate.
